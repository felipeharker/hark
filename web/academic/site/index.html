Abstract

Three projects from the Bachelor of Architecture at the University of Houston’s Hines College of Architecture and Design: a modular housing community in the hills of Rio de Janeiro, an urban design proposal for the neighborhood around Greenspoint Mall, and an adaptive-reuse marketplace in Houston’s Near Northside. Each has a page of its own with its drawings.
