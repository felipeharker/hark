Abstract

Every page on felipeharker.org is built on one stylesheet. This page renders each of its tokens and components exactly as the other pages receive them. The reasoning is written down in web/standards/docs in the repository; this page is for seeing it.

Colour

A white ground, three steps of warm near-black text, two hairline greys and one deep red. The red marks what can be clicked or what is selected, and nothing else. Change --color-accent and its hover and wash follow.

Type

Inter for anything a person wrote; IBM Plex Mono for anything read off a record. Three weights — 400, 500, 600 — and nothing bolder. Each sample below is set at the size it names; sizes step down below 640px.
